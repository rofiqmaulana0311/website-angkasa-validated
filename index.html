The studio.

Angkasa Studio is an independent design studio working across brand, product and content.

We partner with founders and in-house teams on positioning, identity systems and the digital products that carry them. We stay small on purpose: a handful of projects at a time, with the people who sell the work also doing it.

Everything we make is built to be handed over. Systems your own team can run, files they trust, and decisions written down so the work keeps holding together after we leave.
